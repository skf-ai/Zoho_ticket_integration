# Logging in to the LMS

The learning portal is at https://lms.siddhantaknowledge.org/. **Most students
(about 90%) sign in with "Login with Gmail"** — click that button on the login
page and sign in using their registered Gmail address (the email ID given by
the college at enrolment, e.g. `abcd@gmail.com`). A smaller number of accounts
use a traditional Moodle username/password instead.

## Cannot log in / "Login with Gmail" not working

1. Confirm they are clicking **"Login with Gmail"**, not trying to type a
   separate LMS password — most accounts have no separate password at all.
2. Confirm the Gmail account they are signing in with is the **exact** one
   registered with the college. A different personal Gmail account will not
   work even if it looks similar.
3. If Google shows an error or asks for permission, have them approve/allow
   access when prompted — declining the permission blocks sign-in.
4. Try a different browser, or a private/incognito window, and clear the cache.

If the student has done all of the above and still cannot log in, this needs
the LMS admin — raise a ticket. Common causes that only an admin can fix: the
account was never activated, the registered email on file is wrong or
outdated, or the account is suspended.

## For the smaller number of accounts using a Moodle username/password

1. Check the username is exactly as it appears in the welcome email. It is
   usually the registered email address, not a nickname or roll number.
2. Passwords are case-sensitive. Ask the student to check Caps Lock, and to type
   the password rather than paste it (pasting often carries a trailing space).
3. Use the **Forgot password** link on the login page. A reset link goes to the
   registered email address and is valid for a limited time.
4. If the reset email does not arrive, check the spam and promotions folders and
   search the inbox for "https://lms.siddhantaknowledge.org/" or "password".

## "Invalid login, please try again" repeatedly

Moodle temporarily locks an account after several failed attempts. The lockout
clears on its own after a short wait. If the student is certain the password is
correct and it still fails after waiting, raise a ticket — the password may need
an admin reset.

## Account locked

Repeated failed attempts lock the account. Waiting usually clears it. If the
student is blocked and cannot wait (for example during an exam), raise a ticket
and mark it urgent in the description.
